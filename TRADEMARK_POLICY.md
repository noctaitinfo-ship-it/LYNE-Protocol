**official LYNE project, operated by Margus Šuman / Nocta OÜ (Estonia)**.

- **Not granted by any license.** The documentation license (CC BY 4.0) and the code license (Apache-2.0) **do not
  grant trademark rights** to the name, the logo, any certification mark, or any confusingly similar name or
  branding.
- **No registration claimed.** This policy states how the name may be used; it does not claim a registered
  trademark. No registration is claimed here unless one exists and is named.

## Allowed without asking: truthful, nominative reference

You may use the name in plain text to say truthfully what your work does. For example:

- "Implements LYNE Protocol 0.1"
- "Compatible with LYNE Protocol 0.1"
- "Passes the LP 0.2 candidate-4 conformance vectors for the modules lp02.federation, lp02.admission"
- "A client for the LYNE Protocol"
- "Based on the LYNE Protocol specification"

Such a statement must be accurate: the version must be named, and it must be true for that version (`CONFORMANCE.md`
describes how to test). Do not use the LYNE logo or visual identity to make it.

## Not allowed without written permission

- **Claims of official status or approval**, such as "Official LYNE", "Official LYNE implementation", "LYNE certified",
  "Approved by LYNE", "Endorsed by LYNE", or anything suggesting the official project approved, certified or operates
  your work.
- **Using the LYNE logo, symbol, wordmark or lockup**, or a modified or look-alike version of them, for your product,
  service, website or app icon.
- **A confusingly similar name**: a product, project, company or domain name that contains "LYNE" or a confusingly
  similar name, used for communication services or software.
- **Calling a modified specification "LYNE".** A changed version of the specification, distributed as LYNE Protocol
  or as an LP version, can be confused with an official version. A fork must use its own name, and may say it is
  "derived from the LYNE Protocol".

There is **no certification program**. Any claim of LYNE certification is false.

## Official versions

Only versions released through [`GOVERNANCE.md`](GOVERNANCE.md) are "the official LYNE Protocol". Signed official
release tags (`release/SIGNING.md`) are how to tell them apart.

## Permission and reports

To request permission, or to report misuse, open an issue in this repository. For anything confidential, use the
private reporting channel in [`SECURITY.md`](SECURITY.md).
