# Patent policy: no separate pledge

**No separate LYNE-wide patent pledge, non-assertion covenant or patent license has been made.**

The only patent terms that apply are those in the licenses themselves:

- **Apache-2.0 §3**, for the Apache-licensed files (schemas, vectors, examples, conformance code). Each contributor
  grants a patent license for its contributions as that section states, and the grant ends for anyone who brings
  patent litigation as it states.
- **CC BY 4.0 §2(b)(2)**, for the specification text. Patent rights are **not** licensed by it.

Nothing in this repository is a promise or commitment about patents beyond those licenses. Any future patent policy
would be a separate, explicit decision of the Protocol Steward, published as such.

## Legal review status

No legal or patent review of this publication has been performed. LP 0.1 Appendix B (B.6) asks for a patent-counsel
review before a public release; the author decided to publish without it. No freedom-to-operate is claimed.

The specification text keeps Appendix B unchanged (LP 0.1 is immutable). This section is the current status of
that item for this repository. It is a statement of fact, not legal advice: anyone implementing the protocol makes
their own assessment.
